## 🧩 About

**Halo Campaign Evolved Companion** is a curated list of Halo Campaign Evolved open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, overlay viewer, waypoint switcher
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — loadout catalog, armor notes, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — mission layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Mission Helpers
- Waypoint Switcher — mark objectives from any checkpoint
- overlay Viewer — visualize nearby units and pickups
- Run Notes — record and review mission runs
- Playback Speed — adjust review playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Armor Catalog — browse armor pieces, colors, visors, effects
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize spartan cosmetics
- HUD Clarify — improved objective and compass visibility

### 🏋️ Practice Layout
- Checkpoint Notes — segment study on any mission
- Movement Control Notes — manual stance and sprint toggles
- Combo Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the sandbox
